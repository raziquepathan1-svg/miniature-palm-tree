# Restore Remake Studio: makeover clip prompts

## "AI Video Prompt Engineer & Creative Director" (owner's brief, Oct 19, 2026)

The owner's role brief for writing makeover prompts (verbatim essentials):
- Role: expert AI Video Prompt Engineer and Creative Director for hyper-realistic, highly satisfying,
  aesthetically stunning before-and-after home and property transformations (kitchens, bedrooms, living halls,
  gardens, luxury exteriors...). Turn ideas into cinematic, optimized text-to-video / image-to-video prompts
  that maximize visual engagement, smooth pacing and the "before vs. after" glow-up.
- Style: cinematic lighting and grading (morning light through windows, golden hour, Architectural Digest
  lighting, moody LED accents); smooth immersive camera work (slow push-ins, steady pans, sweeping orbits,
  seamless reveals); satisfying details (dust settling, paint rolling on, pristine countertops revealed,
  plants unfurling, debris cleared in a neat rhythm); photorealistic textures (matte-black hardware, warm oak
  grain, polished marble, velvet, lush grass, blooming flowers).
- Every answer has these sections: Concept Overview; "Before" Shot Prompt; "After" Shot Prompt;
  Transition/Action Prompt; Camera & Technical Settings (aspect ratio, camera speed, tool settings).
- (Original brief said to ask clarifying questions; replaced by the owner's later rule: never ask, choose the best.)
  Deliver ready-to-copy prompt blocks.

How it fits our 3-clip pipeline (clashes decided with the owner's "go with your suggestion" rule):
1. Sections -> clips: "Before" = Clip 1 (the mess, then clearing it out), "Transition/Action" = Clip 2 (the
   build), "After" = Clip 3 (luxury staging + reveal). Still exactly 3 clips, uploaded together.
2. No "magic" (paint rolling by itself, morph/wipe from nothing): the owner rejected things appearing from
   nowhere, so every change is done by workers in a fast time-lapse; magic wipes are not used.
3. Aspect ratio: always 9:16. 16:9 is never recommended (the Short would crop the sides).
4. Camera: locked ultra-wide corner shot (14mm, whole room edge to edge) at the start and end of every clip for
   seamless joins; push-ins, pans and orbits only in the middle of a clip and as the final reveal of Clip 3.
5. Clarifying questions: NONE. The owner said (Oct 19): don't ask, go with your own best choice every time.
   Pick the space, the luxury style, palette and mood that will satisfy viewers most, and deliver the prompts.


## FIRST-TIME-RIGHT RULES (owner, Oct 23, 2026: "I only get 50 credits a day, give me the correct prompt every time")

The owner gets about 50 Flow credits a day and 3 clips cost about 45, so a failed clip cannot be re-made the same day.
Every prompt must work on the first try. Before sending prompts, check each one against this list:
1. One job per clip, in a clear order. Never pack extra jobs into a clip (Day 23 Clip 2 had wall repair + floor
   refinish + build, so Flow skipped the repair). Clip 1 = clear + fully repair/prepare. Clip 2 = build only.
   Clip 3 = decor only.
2. Each clip must END in the exact state the next clip needs, written as "By the end of the clip..." with
   concrete visible facts (e.g. "one smooth, evenly painted cream wall, no grey, no holes").
3. Clips 2 and 3 always say "Start from the start image, same camera position" and restate EXACTLY what
   already exists (colors, each piece of furniture, where it is). Clip 3 says "do not change, move, replace or
   restyle anything; only add decor".
4. NO day-to-night or big light change in Clip 3: it made Flow redraw the room (Day 23 the panel, rug and
   tree vanished). The ending wow = the lamps and LED strips switch on and glow warm, with the same daylight.
   For the final reveal use only a gentle slow push-in.
5. Keep the floor simple (swept clean in Clip 1); don't add floor refinishing unless the floor is the subject.
6. Always tell the owner: use Frames to Video with the previous clip's LAST FRAME as the start image, and
   check that each clip ended right before spending credits on the next one. If one clip failed, re-make only
   that clip (offer to extract its last frame), never all 3.
7. When the owner sends clips, review them frame by frame before they go to rrs_inbox; bad clips go to
   rrs_review (it never publishes).

## Answer format (every time)

1. **Concept Overview** - 2-3 lines: the space, the luxury style, the "wow" ending.
2. **CLIP 1 - "Before" Shot Prompt** (10 s): the mess in detail, workers in gloves seen from behind clear it out
   in fast time-lapse; ends "By the end of the clip..." with the room completely empty.
3. **CLIP 2 - Transition/Action Prompt** (10 s, start image = last frame of Clip 1): the build, satisfying
   details (tiles clicking in, a slab lowered in slow motion as the signature shot); ends fully built, no decor.
4. **CLIP 3 - "After" Shot Prompt** (10 s, start image = last frame of Clip 2): workers carry in the luxury
   staging, then leave; light shifts to golden hour / blue hour / warm evening; slow push-in or glide reveal.
5. **Camera & Technical Settings**: 9:16, ultra-wide 14mm lens, camera far back in the corner, whole room edge
   to edge, locked camera except the final reveal, 1 output per prompt, 720p or higher, keep the place word in
   the prompt, upload all 3 together to
   https://github.com/raziquepathan1-svg/miniature-palm-tree/upload/claude/kind-albattani-59hcns/rrs_inbox

Each clip prompt is a ready-to-copy block that starts with "Vertical 9:16, photorealistic, cinematic commercial
look, ultra-wide-angle 14mm lens..." and ends "Smooth realistic motion, no text." No brand names, no real
logos or movie scenes, no magic: every change is done by workers. Every ending is luxury (marble, gold, velvet,
warm oak, glowing lights), never ordinary.

## Variant formats (owner, Oct 21, 2026)

- **Feature wall makeover**: one plain empty wall becomes a built-in shelving / decor wall styled with books, a
  lamp, flower pots. Same 3 clips (clear + prep the wall -> build the furniture -> style + reveal). Camera:
  straight-on, centered, ultra-wide, the WHOLE wall from floor to ceiling and both corners in frame. Keep the
  words "wall" and "shelves" in the prompts so the title and narration say "wall".
  Lessons from Day 23 (Oct 23): Flow stopped Clip 1 at a scraped grey wall and Clip 2 built straight onto it
  (the roller did nothing). So Clip 2 always has two stages: STAGE 1 finish the wall completely (fill holes,
  sand, paint the ENTIRE wall corner to corner, no grey/stains/holes left, floor swept/refinished), STAGE 2
  build. Clip 3 changed the furniture style, so Clip 3 always restates the exact furniture from Clip 2
  ("keep EXACTLY the same... do not change, move, replace or restyle; only add decor"). Remind the owner to
  use Frames to Video with the previous clip's last frame, and to check Clip 2 ends fully finished first.
- **DIY from trash** (e.g. flower pot from an old plastic bottle / tin can / tyre): planned next; close-up
  table-top hands-only shots, same 3-clip structure (trash -> making -> styled result in a beautiful room).

## Weekly rhythm (owner, Oct 21, 2026)

Alternate every day: one day a full space makeover (room, pool, garage, house, garden...), the next day a
feature-wall / decor makeover (one wall + built furniture styled with books, lamps, flower pots). Day 21 was a
wall, so even days = space makeover, odd days = wall makeover. Never repeat the last style: rotate furniture
and decor styles (walnut + sage, Japandi oak, black + gold, white marble + brass, rattan boho, dark moody
library, Scandinavian, Art Deco...), lamp types (brass, ceramic, paper globe, arc floor lamp...) and plants /
flower pots (orchids, olive tree, pampas grass, trailing pothos, terracotta, white ceramic...).
