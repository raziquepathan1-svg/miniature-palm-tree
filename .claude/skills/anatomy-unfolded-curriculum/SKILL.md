# The teaching ladder (from start to end)
CELL -> TISSUE -> ORGAN -> SYSTEM -> WHOLE BODY. Students first learn what a cell is, its parts (design) and their work, how cells
group into tissues, how tissues build organs, how organs build systems. Then every system is taught ORGAN BY ORGAN:
  1 choose ONE system   2 choose ONE organ of it   3 that organ, in this order:
     a) ANATOMY: where it sits (whole-body picture first) -> outside parts -> inside parts (cut open) -> the CELLS and TISSUES it is
        made of (their design and their work)
     b) PHYSIOLOGY: how the organ works, how its cells do the job
     c) DISEASES: related diseases, what goes wrong, symptoms, complications
     d) PREVENTION: how to prevent them, when to see a doctor
  4 next organ of the same system ... until the system is complete -> then the next system.
Never jump to another system or organ before the current one is finished.

# Rules
1. ONE SYSTEM AT A TIME, in this order, and finish it before starting the next system:
   a) ANATOMY - every part by name, where it is in the body (show it on the whole-body picture first), what is inside it.
   b) PHYSIOLOGY - how each part works and how the parts work together.
   c) DISEASES - the system's common diseases: what goes wrong, symptoms, complications, prevention, when to see a doctor.
2. LABELLED DIAGRAMS in every anatomy scene: each named part gets its NAME on the picture with a pointer line to the exact spot
   (HA.kit `lead` line + text), not only a word list at the side. Inside views (cut-open organ) are required, not just outside views.
   Image prompts still say "no text" (AI text is misspelled); we add the labels ourselves, so leave space and ask for clear parts.
3. LOCATION FIRST: every organ video starts by showing where the organ sits on the whole-body picture (glow + label), then zooms in.
4. Each video ends by naming the NEXT video in the same system order (no jumping to another system).
5. Playlists and the description line "Part of the ___ series" must name the RIGHT system (one playlist per system).
6. Fact-check rules, Shorts, packaging and cinematic style rules of the other anatomy-unfolded skills still apply.

# Course plan
FOUNDATION (first): F1 The cell - parts and their work | F2 Inside the nucleus: DNA -> proteins | F3 How cells make energy (mitochondria)
  | F4 How cells divide (mitosis) | F5 The 4 tissue types (epithelial, connective, muscle, nervous) | F6 Cells -> tissues -> organs -> systems: the body's 11 systems
Then systems, organ by organ (each organ: location+outside, inside, cells+tissues, physiology, diseases, prevention).

# System plan (order)
1 Cardiovascular  2 Respiratory  3 Nervous (+ special senses)  4 Digestive  5 Urinary  6 Endocrine  7 Immune/Lymphatic
8 Musculoskeletal  9 Integumentary (skin)  10 Reproductive.
Cardiovascular series (first):
 Anatomy:    1 Where is the heart? location + outside parts | 2 Inside the heart: 4 chambers + 4 valves | 3 Heart wall + coronary arteries
             4 Blood vessels: arteries, veins, capillaries | 5 Main arteries and veins of the body (aorta and its branches, venae cavae)
 Physiology: 6 Blood flow path (pulmonary + systemic circulation) | 7 The cardiac cycle + heart sounds | 8 The heart's electrical system
             9 Blood pressure, heart rate and cardiac output | 10 Blood: what it is made of
 Diseases:   11 High blood pressure | 12 Atherosclerosis + coronary artery disease | 13 Heart attack | 14 Heart failure
             15 Arrhythmias + AFib | 16 Valve disease | 17 Stroke (from the vessels) | 18 How to protect your heart (prevention)
Earlier videos that fit a slot (heart electrical, heart valves, arrhythmia, blood pressure...) are linked as that slot or remade with labels.
