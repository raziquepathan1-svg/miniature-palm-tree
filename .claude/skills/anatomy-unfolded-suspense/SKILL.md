# Owner's instructions (9 Nov 2026, in the owner's words, tidied)
- People watch our Shorts but do not click through to the full video or subscribe. Give them a REASON.
- "Do something like in movies: create suspense - what will happen next - in our Shorts. Act like a story writer and
  a movie director, and do it for our Reels and for our videos."

# How it is applied
## Shorts / Reels (30-45 s) = one movie scene, 5 beats
1. COLD OPEN (0-3 s): start in the middle of a strange case or question, never "today we learn...".
   (a real patient/case, or a "how is this even possible?" fact)
2. THE CASE (3-15 s): 2-3 short sentences of story. A person, a place/year, what went wrong.
3. COMPLICATION (15-28 s): a "but..." twist that deepens the mystery (the clue that makes it strange).
4. NEAR-REVEAL (28-35 s): point at the answer without saying it ("a part the size of an almond...").
5. CUT-OFF CLIFFHANGER (last 5-8 s): stop BEFORE the answer, name where it is: "The answer is in today's full video
   at 3:30 PM" (12 PM Short) / "...in our full video, live now" (9 PM Short) + the subscribe reason:
   "Subscribe: a new body explainer every day".
- The Short must be 100% true. Suspense = curiosity, never fear or fake danger. No gore. No personal medical advice.
- The promise must be paid off in the full video (the case/answer appears there), otherwise it is clickbait.
- Use real, well-documented cases or clear "how is this possible?" facts; mark uncertain history as "reportedly".
- Series branding on screen: "THE ANATOMY UNFOLDED - CASE FILE" (builds a reason to subscribe).

## Full videos = a movie in acts
- Cold open (first 5-10 s): the same mystery/case, then "let's solve it".
- Chapters are ACTS; plant one open loop mid-video ("hold that thought - we will come back to it") and pay it off.
- Final scene: recap + a TRAILER for the next episode (a new mystery, left open).

## Process
- Scripts (Phase 2 tables) are shown to the owner for approval before rendering (existing owner rule).
- Short ending wording: bridge.py (card + voice) - it must always name the reason: "full answer in the video" +
  "subscribe: new body explainer daily".
